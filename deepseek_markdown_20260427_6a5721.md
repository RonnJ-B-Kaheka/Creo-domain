# Usability Test Plan – Discord

**Website**  
Discord – a free voice, video, and text chat platform.

**Typical Users**  
Gamers, online community members, student study groups, and remote teams who communicate in real time.

---

## Three Realistic Tasks

1. **Join a public server** – Find and join the official “Minecraft” community server, then locate the `#announcements` text channel.
2. **Start a direct voice call** – Open a Direct Message with a friend and initiate a voice call.
3. **Share your screen** – While in a voice call, share your entire screen to show a presentation.

---

## Two Pieces of Data Collected & Why

- **Task completion rate (effectiveness)** – Whether the user successfully finishes each task. This tells us if core functions are actually usable.
- **Time on task (efficiency)** – How long each task takes. Long times often signal confusing layout, hidden buttons, or unclear labels.

---

## Technique Used & Rationale

**Think‑aloud protocol** – Participants speak their thoughts, decisions, and frustrations aloud while performing tasks. This reveals *why* they click certain things, where they expect to find features, and what they misunderstand, giving deeper insight than silent observation alone.

---

## Moderator Checklist

- [ ] Consent form signed and purpose of the session explained  
- [ ] Demonstrate think‑aloud with a short example; confirm microphone and recording setup  
- [ ] Read each task clearly; do **not** give hints  
- [ ] Prompt with “What are you thinking now?” if participant falls silent for more than 10 seconds  
- [ ] Record task completion (pass/fail) and note timestamps  
- [ ] After all tasks, ask 2–3 satisfaction questions (e.g., comfort rating)  
- [ ] Thank participant and offer opportunity to ask questions
